# Procurement domain scope

Public procurement is a first-class future domain for Stödassistenten's business track.

The product should help a company move from ordinary-language business context to relevant public opportunities, procurement readiness, document gaps, structured bid preparation and official-channel handoff without requiring the user to understand procurement jargon in advance.

## Core principles

- situation-first: the business owner describes the company, capacity and goal;
- source-first: the published procurement notice and documents control procurement-specific requirements;
- no win guarantees;
- distinguish general business readiness from procurement-specific mandatory requirements;
- distinguish exclusion grounds, qualification requirements, mandatory requirements, award criteria and contract terms;
- help the supplier understand a no-bid decision when requirements cannot be met;
- support deadline, clarification-question and document reminders;
- use public/historical/synthetic procurement material for early pilots;
- no confidential buyer information or unfair supplier advantage;
- all AI-generated extraction/classification remains reviewable and traceable to source text.

## Target long-term flow

`business situation → opportunity discovery → relevance/risk filter → requirement extraction → readiness gap analysis → document checklist → bid drafting → user approval → official submission handoff → result explanation → learning/watchlist`

This domain shares the same intelligence platform as citizen support: structured situation understanding, verified sources, missing-fact questions, explainable next actions, reminders, multilingual UX and governed learning.
